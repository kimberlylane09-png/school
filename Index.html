JayTee

Bio

JayTee has been producing on the Montreal scene for three years.She started DJing for her friends parties, but she quickly earned a reputation for her entergetic and melodic style, and the demand for her music meant that she was playing shows around town for a growing audience. Shes appeared with artists like Crazed Monarch, Light Without Heart,Distant Sun and Tranquility Race.

Her tracks have been featured on radio in Montreal and Toronto, and she'll be appearing at the Montreal Beat Festival along with Dr. Pephlekshun, who features her sound on his new track, Hate Rebate.
